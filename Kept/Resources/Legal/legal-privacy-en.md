This policy explains what data the Kept app processes, why, for how long and who receives it. It is written to be read in a few minutes.

## 2. The essentials

- Without an account, Kept sends nothing anywhere. Everything stays on your iPhone.
- An account is optional and exists only for friends, bets for points and other shared features. Only the data listed in section 4 goes to the server.
- Your diary, weight, Health data, photos, task notes and reasons for a missed task are never sent to the server.
- We use no advertising, no tracking and no third-party analytics. We do not sell data and we do not train artificial intelligence on it.

## 3. Data that stays on your iPhone

The app stores this data only on your device and we have no access to it:

- tasks, weekly plans, promises, statistics, points and badges,
- the diary (it can be locked with Face ID),
- weight, body measurements and body photos,
- photos used as proof that a task was done,
- workouts, your own exercises and their photos,
- app settings.

With your iPhone's permission the app reads further data. This also stays on the device:

- **Health (Apple Health):** steps, distance, active energy, weight and workouts. Kept only reads them, writes nothing and sends them nowhere.
- **Calendar:** events are read only to warn you about a clash while planning. They are not stored.
- **Camera and photos:** for proof photos, body photos, a photo of your own exercise and for scanning a friend's QR code.
- **Microphone and speech recognition:** only when you dictate a diary entry. Speech is turned into text by a service provided by Apple, and the audio may be sent to Apple's servers in the process. Dictation is off until you turn it on.
- **Face ID:** the check is done by the iPhone. Kept only learns the result.

Body photos and proof photos are excluded from device backups. Other app data may become part of your iPhone backup (iCloud or a computer) if you have backups turned on. Backups are managed by Apple under its own terms.

Widgets, Live Activities and notifications may show task names and numbers on the lock screen.

You can export your data to a file at any time (Settings → Diary and privacy → Export).

## 4. Data we process on the server

We process it only if you create an account.

**Sign-in**

- your e-mail address, if you sign in with a code sent by e-mail,
- when you sign in with Discord, the data Discord passes to us: account identifier, username, e-mail and the address of your profile picture.

**Profile**

- the name you choose, the four-digit number after it and the code for your QR invitation,
- the time you confirmed your age, and the time and version of the terms you accepted. Your date of birth is used only for the age check and is not stored,
- settings: Do Not Disturb and its reason, sharing level, participation in leaderboards, muting of pokes.

**Shared features**

- friendships and friend requests, blocks,
- pokes including their text (up to 100 characters) and reactions,
- weekly numbers: reliability, number of completed and all tasks, streak, number of badges and whether the week is sealed,
- daily numbers (done and total), which your buddies can see,
- your weekly plan (task name, category, day, status), only if you choose to share "The whole weekly plan",
- bets for points, shared challenges, groups, shared activities and witness requests, including the names and texts you write for them,
- in-app notifications,
- reports: the reason, a note and the text of the reported poke.

**Technical data**

- IP address, time and technical details of the request in the operating logs of the server provider,
- records of the number of actions, kept for abuse protection (stored for 2 days).

## 5. Why we process data and on what legal basis

- **Running the account and shared features:** performance of the contract you enter into with us by accepting the terms (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
- **Security, abuse protection and moderation of reported content:** our legitimate interest in a safe service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- **Age check and obligations towards authorities:** legal obligation and legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(c) and (f) GDPR).

We send no marketing messages. We carry out no profiling and no automated decision-making with legal effects. We do not process health data, because it never leaves your device.

## 6. Who sees your data

- **Friends:** your name, your handle with the number, your Do Not Disturb status (the reason only with your consent) and whatever you choose to share: nothing, a weekly summary, or the whole weekly plan.
- **People you send a friend request to, or who send one to you:** your name and initial.
- **Participants in the same bet, challenge, group or activity:** your name and your status in it; in groups and challenges also your numbers.
- **We, as moderator:** the content of a report and the data needed to handle it. We never reveal who reported whom.

## 7. Processors and other recipients

We currently use these providers:

- **Supabase, Inc. (USA):** database and sign-in. Data is stored in an Amazon Web Services data centre in London (United Kingdom). Supabase's sub-processors include in particular Amazon Web Services and Cloudflare.
- **Sendinblue SAS (Brevo, France):** sending the e-mail with the sign-in code. Your e-mail address is passed on.
- **Discord Inc. (USA):** only if you choose to sign in with Discord. Discord is an independent controller and follows its own policy.
- **Apple:** provides the iPhone and its services (Health, speech recognition, backups, notifications). Apple is an independent controller.

Transfers outside the European Economic Area: the United Kingdom is covered by an adequacy decision of the European Commission. Transfers to the USA rely on standard contractual clauses or on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. [TO BE VERIFIED once the data processing agreements are concluded.]

We may disclose data to public authorities where the law requires us to.

**What we are preparing.** We will update this policy before turning on any of these services:

- notifications through the Apple Push Notification service (a device identifier is passed on for delivery),
- Sign in with Apple,
- purchases through the App Store (Apple processes the payment; we do not see payment details),
- syncing of personal data through your iCloud (the data will be in your Apple account and we will have no access to it),

## 8. How long we keep data

- Account, profile and friendships: until the account is deleted.
- Pokes, reactions and notifications: 30 days.
- Daily numbers: 3 days. Weekly numbers: 400 days.
- Shared weekly plan: 60 days.
- Witness requests: 180 days.
- Shared activities: 30 days after their date. Challenges: 60 days after they end.
- Closed bets: 180 days after they are settled.
- Resolved reports: 365 days. Unresolved reports until they are resolved.
- Records of the number of actions: 2 days.
- Operating logs and backups of the server provider: [TO BE VERIFIED: periods under the Supabase plan].

Deleting your account (Settings → Account → Delete account) removes the data on the server at once. Data on your iPhone stays until you delete it or uninstall the app.

## 9. Your rights

You have the right:

- to access your data and receive a copy,
- to rectification (you can change your name in Settings),
- to erasure (by deleting your account in the app or by an e-mail request),
- to restriction of processing,
- to data portability,
- to object to processing based on legitimate interest,
- to lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů), Pplk. Sochora 27, 170 00 Prague 7, www.uoou.cz.

You can withdraw iPhone permissions (Health, calendar, camera, microphone, notifications) at any time in iPhone Settings.

Send your request to the contact in section 1. We will reply without undue delay and within one month at the latest.

## 10. Children

An account is intended for people aged 15 and over. We do not knowingly process data of younger people. If we learn that an account belongs to someone younger, we will delete it.

## 11. Security

The connection to the server is encrypted. In the database everyone sees only what they are entitled to, and sign-in credentials are stored in the Keychain on your iPhone. Photos are encrypted on the device. No security is perfect, however; if an incident occurs that puts you at risk, we will let you know.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We will tell you in the app in advance about any material change. The current wording is always available in Settings.

## 13. Language versions

This policy exists in Czech and in English. In the event of any discrepancy between the language versions, the English version prevails.
